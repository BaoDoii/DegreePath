How it works

Prerequisite validation

Uses graph-based algorithms to check every course requirement automatically. You'll only see courses you're actually eligible to take.

Smart priority ranking

Courses that unlock the most future options are scheduled first. So you're always making the most progress toward graduation.

Multi-semester planning

Generate a complete 1–8 semester graduation roadmap. Set your max units per semester and let DegreePath do the rest.

Workload warnings

Automatically flags semesters with too many heavy courses so you can spread out the workload before it's too late to change.

GE placeholder support

Add General Education courses as placeholders in your plan. Each GE is counted as 3 units toward your semester total.

Full CS curriculum

Covers all 23 CSUEB CS courses — math prerequisites, lower division, upper division core, and breadth electives.
